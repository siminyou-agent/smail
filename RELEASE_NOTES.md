# Smail TestFlight

## 1.0 (6)

### English

- English and Simplified Chinese interface, following the system language by default.
- Choose System Default, 简体中文, or English in Settings → Language. Changes apply immediately and are saved across launches without resetting sorting progress.
- Localized reader controls, accessibility labels, sorting summaries, and app error messages. Email content and Gmail label names stay unchanged.

### 中文

- 新增完整中英文界面，默认跟随系统语言。
- 在「设置 → 语言」选择「跟随系统」、简体中文或 English，即时切换并记住选择，不重置分类进度。
- 阅读器控件、辅助功能标签、分类统计及应用错误提示支持双语。邮件内容和 Gmail 标签名称保持不变。

## 1.0 (5)

### English

First Smail beta: sort Gmail in small batches of ten.

- Connect your Google account directly; no Smail server.
- Get the ten newest unclassified Inbox messages, read or unread.
- Swipe right for Useful, left for Not Useful; changes sync to Gmail labels.
- Full card exit animation, undo, saved progress and retry for interrupted writes.
- Read original HTML emails with optional remote images and tappable links.
- Smail only creates/changes its two custom labels. It never deletes, archives, sends mail or changes read status.

Please test Google sign-in, newest-first ordering, swipe/undo, and email rendering. Google OAuth is currently in Testing mode; your Gmail account must be on the test-user list and may need reauthorization after seven days.

### 中文

Smail 首个测试版：每次十封，轻松给 Gmail 邮件分类。

- 独立 Google 登录，手机直连 Gmail，无 Smail 后台。
- 每批取收件箱最新十封未分类邮件，包含已读和未读。
- 右划有用，左划没用，分类结果同步为 Gmail 标签。
- 完整划出动画、撤销、本机进度保存和失败重试。
- 原始 HTML 正文、按需加载图片和可点击链接。
- 只创建和修改 Smail 的两个自定义标签，不删除、归档、发送邮件，也不修改已读状态。

请测试 Google 登录、时间倒序、划卡／撤销和原文排版。Google 授权目前为 Testing 模式，登录 Gmail 需加入测试用户，并可能每七天重新授权。
