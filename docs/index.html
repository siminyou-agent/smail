YOUR MAIL, YOUR CONTROL

Labels change.
Your mail doesn’t.

Smail connects directly to Google. No Smail server. No AI processing. No deleting, archiving, or changing read status.

Google’s gmail.modify permission is broader than these actions; Smail enforces its two-label limit in code. Remote images stay blocked until you choose to load them.

OPEN SOURCE. MAKE IT YOURS.

Give your inbox a minute.

Clone the repo, open it in Xcode, and try ten demo emails. No Google account needed for the demo.

Requires macOS, Xcode, and XcodeGen. Connecting real Gmail needs your own Google OAuth setup.
